Our Products

🛡️ KithGuard — Personal Safety & Commute Companion

KithGuard is a mobile safety app designed for Nigerian families and commuters. It provides real-time tracking, emergency alerts, and automated notifications so parents and loved ones always know their family is safe.

  • One-tap SOS with live location sharing
  • Trip tracking with destination and ETA
  • Parent-child safety monitoring
  • Automated routine alerts for school runs
  • Live location map for family members
  • Real-time SMS notifications to emergency contacts
